How to sign in & switch roles

  1. Open https://main.d2gei9r9nlt6ui.amplifyapp.com/login
  2. Enter the email and password for the role you want to test.
  3. Click Sign In — you should land in the matching portal.
  4. To switch roles: avatar menu (top-right) → Sign out → log in with another account.

First open after idle may take a few seconds (Amplify cold start). Wait for the login page, then continue.

Role walkthrough

1. Super Admin — what to test

Account: s.alamri@oia.gov.om / OiaDemo#2026

  1. Confirm you land on the Admin overview with platform KPIs / health.
  2. Open Users — list loads; invite / manage UI is available.
  3. Open Roles & Permissions — roles are visible.
  4. Open AI / System health — pages load without errors.
  5. Open Audit trail — logs or a clear empty state.
  6. Confirm a Candidate account cannot open /admin (security check after you switch roles).

Role walkthrough

2. HR Workspace — what to test

Account: hr.demo@oia.gov.om / OiaHr#2026

  1. Confirm landing on /hr/dashboard with KPIs and widgets.
  2. Click Ask AI Copilot — side panel opens (no full-screen blur).
  3. Candidates — open the list, select a row, review the detail split view.
  4. Jobs — select a job; review matches / detail.
  5. Use top-bar + Create to open create flows.
  6. Visit AI Matching, CV Parsing, AI Interviews, Assessments.
  7. Open Analytics and Reports — try CSV/PDF export if available.
  8. Open Calendar and Settings.
  9. Toggle language (EN ↔ AR) if the globe control is present.
  10. Try shortcuts: Ctrl+K search, Ctrl+B sidebar, Ctrl+J AI dock.

Role walkthrough

3. Candidate Portal — what to test

Account: candidate.demo@example.com / OiaCand#2026

  1. Confirm landing on /candidate home.
  2. Open Jobs — browse open roles and apply (or see “already applied”).
  3. Open My applications — pipeline / status is visible.
  4. Check Interviews and Assessments for pending items or empty states.
  5. Update Profile / upload a resume if available — save succeeds.
  6. Open Career Assistant — chat responds.
  7. Sign out and visit /candidate-signup to review public registration (email confirmation may be required).
